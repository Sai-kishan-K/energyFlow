# Stakeholder Discovery Questions

1. Who sends readings, and through what interface?
2. Is `consumption_kwh` an interval delta, or do meters send cumulative readings?
3. Can one event be resent? What identifier defines a duplicate?
4. What happens to readings that fail schema validation?
5. Which fields are mandatory for billing vs for monitoring?
6. How are timestamps and daylight saving time handled?
7. Which tariff plans, effective dates, taxes and rounding rules apply?
8. Who can see identifiable customer usage data?
9. How long must readings and rejection logs be retained?
10. What is the minimum acceptable workflow for the first release?
